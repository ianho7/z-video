## Limits / not proved

- Ticket 01 imports one supported video and derives title from filename. NFO/XML parsing, artwork, incremental/cancellable scans, show/season/episode mapping and unavailable-root behavior belong to Ticket 02.
- Native local playback, progress persistence, fresh-process resume, track preferences and failed-load preservation belong to Ticket 03.
- Synthetic/owned fixtures do not prove real personal media, UNC/share policy, practical NFO compatibility, hardware decode, macOS, DWM, or packaged candidate acceptance.
- The window fixture smoke uses controlled IPC handlers; the separate app smoke launches real `main.ts` but does not automate a native folder dialog.
